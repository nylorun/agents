The Tenant has a page (`/tenant`), opened from the Tenant at the top of the sidebar; the sidebar's Configure section (Manifests, Models) is gone. Its header names the Tenant, its id, the model it runs, its sandbox and how many Agents and sessions it holds, and its ⋯ resets the Tenant's sessions, Agents or everything, each after a confirm. One row of tabs follows, in the order an Agent's turn meets them: **Status** (its checks, what runs it, what it holds), **Manifests** (the manifests list, with one open beside it), **Models**, **Compute** (the sandbox, the backends found, what an Agent may ask for, placement and storage, editable), **Tool policy** (the rules, strictest first, each edited in a panel), **Limits** (the Runtime limits by area beside their defaults, each editable or reset), **Usage** (model usage today, this month and in all, and the budgets, editable) and **Keys** (application and signing keys, read only). An Agent's Manifest tab and a manifest beside the list note the tool policy rules on each HTTP tool and MCP server. `/settings`, `/settings/models` and `/agent-manifests` open the page's tabs. The Studio proxy allows the Tenant's settings reads, their `PUT`s and `POST /v1/tenant/reset`; no key write.
