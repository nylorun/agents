**A better message box in a session's chat.** The text and one round button now sit in a single card, with the model picker under it. The box grows with its text, **Enter** sends and **Shift+Enter** adds a line. While a turn runs the button stops it, taking over from the **Cancel** button in the session header. The chat opens at the newest message, follows new ones while you are at the bottom, and offers a button back to the latest when you scroll up.
