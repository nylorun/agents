Studio follows its DESIGN.md (`studio/DESIGN.md`, in Google Labs' design.md format): shadcn/ui on the Zinc base color, with zinc and white everywhere and color only in charts. Charts take the Sage scale: a session's tokens by kind run from forest to mist, and a budget's used bar is forest on a mist track. State is a grey dot and a word, darkest when something failed or waits on a person; waiting counts are zinc badges, errors are foreground text, and an approval waiting on you is outlined, not amber. Headings are in Space Grotesk, text in DM Sans and ids in JetBrains Mono, bundled with the image.
