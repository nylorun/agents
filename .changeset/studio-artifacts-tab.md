A session's Outputs tab is now Artifacts, since it also holds the files a client uploads. It lists each artifact as a row (type, a version badge past v1, time) and opens one in place of the list, with a way back and the previous or next artifact, so its preview is no longer pushed below the list. The inspector puts the preview first under a one-line summary (type, size, source, time) and folds the IDs, owner and hash into Details; its refresh buttons are icons.
