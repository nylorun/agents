An Agent's page is redesigned as a dashboard of the manifest version it is an instance of. Its header names that version (tracking or pinned), its kind, owner and labels, with **New session** and a ⋯ menu (Edit labels and owner, Stop compute, Reset disk, Fork, Delete). It opens on **Sessions**, then **Triggers**, **Artifacts**, **Memory**, **Disk** and **Activity**, and on the right **Vault** and **Manifest**: each tab is one full-width table of single-line rows with a search, filter menus and a count, row actions behind ⋯, and a row's details in a panel on the right. **Triggers** (pause, resume, run now, and every run) and **Memory** (each memory's content and history, and delete) are new; the proxy now allows `GET …/triggers`, `…/runs`, `…/memories`, `…/memories/{path}`, `…/memory_versions` and `…/memory_versions/{id}`, `POST …/triggers/{id}/pause|resume|run` and `DELETE …/memories/{path}`. **Vault** lists the slots the manifest declares; setting one asks only for its value. **Manifest** reads the version as a spec, its harness section then its runtime section, with the moves (Pin, Track, Move to v<n>, Restart, Retry) in its ⋯ menu. The Overview, Secrets, Setup & files and Events tabs are gone: `?tab=overview`, `secrets`, `setup` and `events` open Sessions, Vault, Manifest and Activity, and old Credentials and vault links open the Vault.
