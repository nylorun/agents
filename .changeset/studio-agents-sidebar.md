A simpler Studio sidebar. Agents is one flat list, one row per Agent with its state's dot, and a count only when its sessions wait on a person or fail. Agents opens every session, and an Agent filters the session table to its own sessions, as the table's Agent filter does. The table links to the Agent's page (Agent details) or, unfiltered, to the Agents list (Manage Agents). The Sessions entry and the manifest rows under Agents are gone; Manifests and Models sit under Configure at the foot of the sidebar, and New session lists the Agents without grouping them by manifest.
